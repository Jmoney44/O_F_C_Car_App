# Sample car photos

These photos illustrate the sample cars in demo mode. They come from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) and were resized and
cropped to 1280×800 (some have blurred strips added above and below to fit).
Each remains under its original licence; photos marked CC BY-SA are shared
here under that same licence.

They are sample content only. Remove this folder (and its entry in
`pubspec.yaml`) before releasing the app if you do not want to ship them.
